## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a serene intersection between Scandinavian clarity and Japanese architectural restraint (Japandi minimalism). Created for modern remote professionals navigating career transitions and intentional lifestyle planning, it reframes high-stress workflows—such as job hunting and relocation scouting—into a calming, deliberate, and elevated editorial experience.

### Visual Character & Philosophy
- **Restraint and Tactility:** Heavy reliance on warm off-white canvases, micro-borders, and tactile structural grids rather than loud gradients or artificial drop shadows.
- **Dual-Purpose Synergy:** Seamlessly bridges corporate operational utility (pipelines, deadlines, interview notes) with soulful discovery (ambient cafes, third places, quiet library work spots).
- **Cognitive Ease:** High whitespace ratios, strict typographic hierarchies, and muted organic color cues reduce search fatigue and cognitive overwhelm.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from stone gardens, linen stationery, dried tea leaves, and oiled hinoki wood. It avoids synthetic, eye-straining saturations in favor of grounded, earthbound tonalities.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary (`#2D5A46`) — Deep Muted Sage:** Represents focus, balance, and quiet progress. Applied to active states, decisive primary actions, key status milestones, and confirmed job leads.
- **Secondary (`#E6E2DD`) — Warm Sand:** Provides an organic foundational fill for secondary containers, chips, subtle dividers, and inactive state fills.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`) — Warm Muted Amber:** Dedicated to urgency, approaching interview deadlines, upcoming follow-ups, and ambient discovery badges (e.g., "Peak Sunlit Hours").
- **Neutral (`#18181B`) — Deep Charcoal Slate:** Replaces harsh pure black for high-contrast primary typography and sharp structural elements.

### Supporting Canvas & Border Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBF9` (Alabaster Linen) ensures an effortless, paper-like reading environment.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio) for elevated cards, modal sheets, and active interactive elements.
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** `#F4F4F0` (Pressed Stone) for search drawers, sidebars, and nested pipeline columns.
- **Structural Borders:** `#E4E4E0` and `#D4D4CE` for hair-thin, 1px micro-borders separating discrete functional zones.
- **Subtle Muted Text:** `#71717A` (Zinc 500) for metadata, timestamp labels, and supporting architectural specs.

## Typography

The typographic pairing unites the contemporary, human geometric precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian craft of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Roles & Execution
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with negative letter tracking to yield an authoritative yet quiet magazine presence.
- **Long-form Reading & Field Data:** Plus Jakarta Sans body sizes prioritize open counters and natural proportional balance to support scanning interview dossiers and detailed neighborhood work amenities.
- **Monospace Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Reserved for technical and operational anchors: pipeline stages (`STAGE // 03`), salary expectations, Wi-Fi speed benchmarks (`180 MBPS`), noise-level metrics, and precise application deadlines. Monospace tags should be rendered in uppercase or small-caps with slight tracking for technical elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system deploys a structured, disciplined grid governed by architectural margins and breathable breathing room.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid layout maxing out at `1440px` centered canvas. Columns are separated by `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px) with canvas margins of `margin: 2.5rem` (40px) to prevent crowded viewport boundaries.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid layout between `768px` and `1024px` with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single primary column flow with sticky headers, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem` (12px), and `margin-mobile: 1rem` (16px).

### Spacing Principles
Component density alternates deliberately:
- **Operational Kanban & Data Tables:** Dense spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`) for maximum information clarity.
- **Editorial Guides & Spot Cards:** Expressive vertical margins (`space-xl` and beyond) to cultivate an unhurried, calm tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects heavy, synthetic drop shadows. Instead, visual hierarchy and three-dimensional stacking are communicated through **tonal paper layering** and **low-contrast micro-borders**.

### Surface Hierarchy & Layering
1. **Canvas Layer (`#FBFBF9`):** The master ambient backdrop representing natural raw paper.
2. **Structural Recessed Containers (`#F4F4F0`):** Used for sidebar rails, filter drawers, and pipeline track backgrounds. Borders are omitted or kept to `#EAEAEC`.
3. **Card & Component Plinths (`#FFFFFF`):** Work tracking tickets, curated venue reviews, and dialogs sit on pure white tiles bound by a crisp `1px solid #E4E4E0` border.

### Ambient Micro-Shadows
When floating modals, quick-action sheets, or active drag-and-drop pipeline cards must elevate above the canvas, use an imperceptible warm slate ambient diffusion:
- `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.02);`
- Hover states do not lift drastically; rather, their border sharpens to `#2D5A46` or surfaces shift subtly in brightness.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects understated architectural joinery: precise, refined, and subtly rounded without being bulbous or child-like.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to badges, monospace metadata chips, form inputs, and small button controls.
- **Container Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to pipeline cards, spot discovery cards, dropdown panels, and inner container viewports.
- **Large Structural Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved for modal overlays, drawer sheets, and hero media containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#2D5A46`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with `0.25rem` radius. Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Transitions smoothly to `#234737` on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent or `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #D4D4CE` micro-border and `#18181B` text. Hover shifts background to `#F4F4F0`.
- **Text & Editorial Links:** Underlined baseline with `1px solid #D97706` or `#2D5A46` offset by 3px; no background box.

### Chips & Monospace Status Badges
- Built with `JetBrains Mono` at `label-sm`.
- **Interview / Next Steps:** Tinted `#FEF3C7` background with `#D97706` text and border `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **Offer Received / Work Verified:** Tinted `#E6F0EB` background with `#2D5A46` text and border `rgba(45, 90, 70, 0.2)`.
- **Amenity / Spec Badges:** `#F4F4F0` background with `#71717A` text, unbordered or with hairline `#E4E4E0`.

### Form Inputs & Select Controls
- Built on a pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle `1px solid #D4D4CE` outline and `0.25rem` corner radius.
- Height standard: `40px` with `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
- Focused state: Border transforms to `#2D5A46` accompanied by an ultra-thin, low-opacity highlight ring (`rgba(45, 90, 70, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Compact `16px x 16px` squares (`2px` radius) or circles with a `1.5px` border in `#D4D4CE`.
- Checked state fills with `#2D5A46` and displays a crisp white internal check icon.

### Cards: Pipeline Tracker Cards
- `#FFFFFF` surface framed with `1px solid #E4E4E0`.
- Interior layout includes top-aligned company metadata in `label-sm`, title in `headline-sm`, and an amber or sage status indicator at the card footer.
- Hover interaction triggers an intentional border shift from `#E4E4E0` to `#D4D4CE` alongside an optional 2px sage accent line on the left perimeter.

### Cards: Curated Remote Spot Cards
- Aspect ratio-controlled visual frame (16:10) with an image corner radius of `0.375rem`.
- Overlaid badges in monospace (`WI-FI: 240 MBPS`, `NATURAL LIGHT: HIGH`, `NOISE: LOW`).
- Card body features title in `headline-md`, physical district location in `body-sm` (`#71717A`), and quiet micro-tags denoting power outlet abundance and quiet rooms.
